Implement preference setup, optional preference changes, and candidate proposals. Keep the supplied communication wrapper intact.

Add your bot to bots.local.json, then run:

python3 start.py match "My Bot" "Sample A" \
  --bots bots.local.json --n 20

Test n=20, n=100 and n=200. Your bot must play both P and M.
